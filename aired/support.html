Support

Aired · Last updated 29 May 2026

Aired is a shared mental load app for couples and families. This page answers the most common questions. If you can't find what you need, email us at support.nosastudio@gmail.com and we'll get back to you, typically within two working days.

Getting started

How do I sign in?

Aired uses a magic-link sign-in: enter your email, tap the link in the message you receive, and you're in. There is no password to remember. If the email doesn't arrive within a minute or two, check your spam folder and make sure you typed the address correctly.

How do I invite my partner?

Open Settings → Space and tap Share invite code. You can send the code via WhatsApp, copy the link, or use the system share sheet. Your partner enters the six-character code (or taps the link) and lands directly in your shared Space.

My partner's name doesn't appear next to claimed items

Each member can set their display name in Settings → Profile. Until both members set a display name, items will show generic labels.

Using the App

What's the difference between "Clear" and "I've got this"?

"I've got this" claims an item — you're committing to handle it, and your partner sees who's on it. "Clear" moves the item out of the in-air pool entirely; use it when an item is no longer relevant. The Deload session at the end of the week walks you through clearing in batches.

Can I hide items from my partner?

Items added to a shared Space are visible to all members of that Space — that's the core mechanic of Aired, so partners can pick things up without being asked. If you want a private list, create a Solo Space; nobody else can join a Solo Space.

How do I leave a Space?

Email us at support.nosastudio@gmail.com with the subject line "Leave Space" and your account email. We'll remove you from the Space within two working days. (An in-app "Leave Space" control is on the roadmap.)

Subscriptions

What do I get with a paid plan?

The free tier is enough to share a single Solo Space. Paid plans (Solo, Duo, Family) unlock the shared pool, real-time partner sync, the Deload session, and unlimited items.

How do I cancel my subscription?

  • iOS: open the Settings app → tap your name → Subscriptions → Aired → Cancel Subscription. You'll keep access until the end of the current billing period.
  • Android: open the Google Play Store app → tap your profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → Aired → Cancel subscription.
  • In-app shortcut: Settings → Subscription → Manage subscription opens the right page automatically.

How do I restore a purchase?

Open Settings → Subscription → Restore purchases. Make sure you're signed into the same App Store or Google Play account that bought the subscription.

Data and privacy

How do I export my data?

Open Settings → Data & Privacy → Export my data. The export is a CSV containing your items (in-air and cleared). You can save it to Files (iOS) or Downloads (Android) and open it in any spreadsheet app.

How do I delete my account?

Open Settings → Data & Privacy → Delete account. This removes your local data immediately and triggers server-side deletion of your account and authored items within 30 days. If you also want past items you authored removed from a partner's view, mention that in a follow-up email; we handle those requests by hand.

Where is my data stored?

Your account and items are stored in Supabase's EU region. Analytics are sent to PostHog's EU region. Full detail in our Privacy Policy.

Troubleshooting

My partner can't see an item I added

Pull-to-refresh on the Pool screen. If the item still doesn't appear, your partner may be on a separate Space. Open Settings → Space → Invite code on your device, and have your partner check that the same code is shown on theirs. If they differ, one of you has joined a different Space; email us and we'll help merge.

The App is offline / "no internet"

Aired needs an internet connection to sync items between Space members. You can still browse your Pool in read-only mode while offline; new items you add will sync when you're back online.

I'm seeing the same item twice

This usually means an item was added on two devices simultaneously while one was offline. Tap the duplicate and Clear it; the other stays in the pool.
