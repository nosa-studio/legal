Privacy Policy

Aired · Effective 29 May 2026 · Last updated 29 May 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Nosa Studio ("we", "us", "our") handles personal data when you use the Aired mobile application ("the App") for iOS and Android, distributed via the Apple App Store and Google Play. We are based in Switzerland, and we comply with the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) for users in the European Economic Area as well as the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (revFADP) for users in Switzerland.

Aired is a shared mental load app for couples and families. You write down the things on your mind into a shared Space, and the people in that Space — typically your partner — can voluntarily claim them. The App is built around two ideas worth flagging up front: your account uses your email for sign-in (via a one-tap magic link), and the items you add to a shared Space are visible to the other members of that Space. We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties for marketing or advertising.

2. Data We Collect

2.1 Email address (for sign-in)

When you create an account, we collect your email address so that we can send you a magic-link sign-in email. The magic link is a one-time URL that signs you into the App when tapped; we do not store passwords. Your email is processed by our authentication provider, Supabase, in their EU region.

2.2 Items and notes you write (your shared pool)

The core feature of Aired is a shared pool of items. When you add an item, the App stores the item's title, any notes you attach, the auto-detected category, the timestamps, and the current status (in-air, claimed, done). This data is stored in our database hosted by Supabase in the EU region, and is synchronized to the devices of the other members of the same Space so they can see and pick items up.

Partner visibility: items you add to a shared Space are visible to all current members of that Space, along with who added them, who claimed them, and who cleared them. Items are not visible to people outside your Space. Aired does not have public feeds or social sharing.

2.3 Profile data you choose to add

You may optionally provide a display name shown to other members of your Space (e.g. "Maya" next to items you claimed). You may also choose a Space name. Both are optional and editable at any time in Settings.

2.4 In-app purchase data

When you subscribe to a paid tier or restore a previous purchase, the transaction is processed by Apple In-App Purchase on iOS or Google Play Billing on Android. We use RevenueCat to manage subscription state across both platforms. We receive a purchase token and entitlement status that allow the App to verify your subscription; we do not see or store your payment card details.

2.5 Anonymous usage analytics

The App uses PostHog (EU region) to collect anonymous usage analytics that help us understand which features are used and where users encounter issues. The data sent is anonymous and contains no personally identifying information (no email, no item contents, no display name, no precise location). Examples of events we may collect:

  • Onboarding completion
  • Selected Space type (Solo / Duo / Family)
  • Anonymous feature interaction counters (item added, item claimed, item cleared)
  • Crash and error diagnostics

PostHog assigns a randomly generated identifier to each install. This identifier is reset when the App's data is cleared or the App is uninstalled.

3. How We Use Data

  • To sign you in: sending you a magic-link email and validating it when you tap it.
  • To provide the core feature: storing the items in your shared pool and synchronizing them to the other members of your Space so they can see and pick them up.
  • To verify purchases: processing App Store / Play Billing purchase tokens via RevenueCat to enable paid features for subscribers.
  • To improve the App: analyzing aggregated, anonymous usage patterns to fix bugs and prioritize features.
We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or automated decision-making with legal effects. We do not sell your personal data. We do not read the contents of your items for marketing purposes.

4. Sharing Within Your Space

Aired is built around the idea that the things on your mind become visible to the people you choose to share with — usually your partner. To keep this transparent:

  • Every member of your Space sees every item in that Space, including who added it, who claimed it, and who cleared it.
  • Members of your Space do not see your email address, your subscription tier, or your billing data.
  • Invite codes (six characters) are how people join your Space. Anyone with a valid, unexpired invite code can join until you regenerate it. You can regenerate the code at any time in Settings to revoke pending invites.
  • If you leave a Space, your historical items remain visible to the remaining members (because they are part of their shared pool), but you no longer have access to that Space.

5. Third-Party Services

The App relies on the following processors:

5.1 Supabase (Supabase Inc.)

Supabase provides our authentication, database, and real-time synchronization. Your email, your items, and your Space membership are stored in our Supabase project hosted in the EU region. Supabase's privacy policy is available at: supabase.com/privacy.

5.2 RevenueCat (RevenueCat, Inc.)

RevenueCat manages subscription entitlements across the App Store and Google Play. RevenueCat receives the platform purchase token and the anonymous RevenueCat user identifier; it does not receive your email or your items. RevenueCat's privacy policy: revenuecat.com/privacy.

5.3 Apple App Store and Google Play

The App Store and Google Play handle distribution, in-app purchase processing, and subscription billing. Apple's and Google's processing of your payment and account information is governed by their respective privacy policies: apple.com/legal/privacy and policies.google.com/privacy.

5.4 PostHog (PostHog Inc.)

PostHog is the analytics provider we use for anonymous product analytics and crash diagnostics. Our PostHog instance is configured to operate in the EU region without collecting personally identifying information. PostHog's privacy policy is available at: posthog.com/privacy.

6. Data Retention

  • Email address and account: retained while your account is active. Deletion of your account erases your email from our authentication database within 30 days.
  • Items and notes: retained while your account or Space is active. Items you "clear" remain visible in the Done list of your Space for the rolling window the App displays, then are purged. If you delete your account, items you authored are removed; items authored by other Space members remain in their shared pool.
  • Magic-link tokens: short-lived. Each magic link expires after a limited window (typically one hour) and can only be used once.
  • Subscription / purchase tokens: retained for as long as your subscription is active, and for up to 24 months thereafter for accounting and audit purposes.
  • Anonymous analytics: retained by PostHog for up to 12 months, then aggregated or deleted.

7. Your Rights (GDPR / revFADP)

If you are located in the EEA, the UK, or Switzerland, you have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Right of access — to confirm whether we process your data and obtain a copy.
  • Right to rectification — to correct inaccurate data we hold about you.
  • Right to erasure ("right to be forgotten") — to request deletion of your data.
  • Right to restriction of processing — to limit how we process your data.
  • Right to data portability — to receive your data in a machine-readable format.
  • Right to object — to processing based on legitimate interests.
  • Right to withdraw consent — at any time, where processing is based on consent.
  • Right to lodge a complaint — with a supervisory authority. In Switzerland, the Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner (FDPIC); in the EU, your local Data Protection Authority.

The most direct way to exercise erasure is to use Settings → Delete account in the App. You can also export your items as CSV via Settings → Export my data. For other rights, or if you need help, contact us at the address below.

8. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has used the App in a way that resulted in data being collected, please contact us so we can remove the relevant data.
